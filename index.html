Splitify — Zero-Backend Shared Expense Calculator

Fast, private, browser-first shared expense calculator for trips, dinners, outings, and roommate expenses.

Key Features

  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: All data stays in your browser (IndexedDB).
  • 4 Flexible Split Methods: Equal, Percentage (%), By Shares, and Custom Rupee amounts.
  • Greedy Debt Simplification: Reduces 15 transactions to the absolute minimum payment steps.
  • Progressive Web App (PWA): Works completely offline with zero internet access required.
  • 24-Hour Temporary Share Links: Share trip data securely without central user accounts.
