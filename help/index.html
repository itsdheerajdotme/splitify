Help & User Guide — Splitify

Step-by-step guide on creating trips, adding expenses across 4 split methods, installing home screen app, and working 100% offline.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Creating a Trip & Adding Friends

Click New Session / Trip from the home dashboard. Enter a title and member names to launch your trip workspace.

2. 4 Expense Split Methods

Choose between Equal Split, Percentage (%), By Shares, or Custom Rupee Amounts.

3. Debt Simplification

Splitify's algorithm calculates minimum direct payments needed to settle everyone's net position cleanly.

4. Offline Progressive Web App (PWA)

Install Splitify on Android, iPhone (Safari Add to Home Screen), or Desktop for offline expense tracking without internet.
